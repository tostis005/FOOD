# Quinnoa — Proposed article topics 916–1115

This file contains proposed new article topics for numbers 916 through 1115.

It is intentionally isolated on the `new-topics-916-plus` branch and must not be treated as canonical until approved and merged into `main`.

Goals:
- expand the Wagyu cluster with informational, comparison, buying, authenticity, cut, cooking, storage and nutrition intents that can support future commerce;
- add high-intent non-Wagyu gaps without duplicating the existing 001–915 topic inventory;
- preserve one clear primary search intent per article to reduce cannibalization.
