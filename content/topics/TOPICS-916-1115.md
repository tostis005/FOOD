# Quinnoa — Proposed article topics 916–1115

This file contains the reviewed proposed article topics for numbers 916 through 1115.

It is intentionally isolated on the `new-topics-916-plus` branch and must not be treated as canonical until approved and merged into `main`.

Review criteria:
- each topic targets a distinct primary search intent;
- topics were checked against the existing 001–915 inventory to avoid direct duplication and material cannibalization;
- Wagyu is intentionally limited to broad, high-intent informational, comparison, buying, authenticity, cooking and myth questions rather than one page per cut;
- non-Wagyu topics focus on everyday food questions, storage, safety, cooking science, labels, nutrition and visible food anomalies;
- broad existing guides and new long-tail topics may be internally linked, but should not target the same primary query.
